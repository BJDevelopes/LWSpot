How to open LWSpot

1. Drag LWSpot onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open LWSpot from Applications (Launchpad, Spotlight or Finder).

The first time, macOS may say "LWSpot" Not Opened, because LWSpot is not
yet notarized by Apple. That happens only once:

  macOS 15 Sequoia or later
    - Click Done.
    - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
    - Scroll down to Security. Next to "LWSpot was blocked to protect your
      Mac", click Open Anyway, enter your Mac password, then Open Anyway.

  macOS 14 Sonoma or earlier
    - In Applications, Control-click (or right-click) LWSpot, choose Open,
      then Open.

LWSpot needs nothing else installed. Sign in with your Spotify account;
playing music needs Spotify Premium.
